### Gaps found (not fixed, outside this task's files)
- **LGPL-2.1 text is not shipped.** The upstream gzstream files point to the LGPL but include no copy of it, and neither the repository nor the image holds one. The notices give the GNU URL. To meet section 6 strictly, add the text, for example as `LICENSES/LGPL-2.1.txt`, and copy it into the image.
- **The image carries no notices for the binaries or the bundle.** The `Dockerfile` copies `ScanTape`, `web/dist` and the server code into `/app`, but not `LICENSE`, `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` or `third_party/AtomTapeUtils/LICENSE.txt`. The MIT terms of AtomTapeUtils and React, and the LGPL notice for gzstream, require the notice to travel with distributed copies. A one-line `COPY LICENSE THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md third_party/AtomTapeUtils/LICENSE.txt /app/licenses/` would close this gap. The Python packages already carry their own license files in `dist-info`.
- **httpx, httpcore and certifi are test-only but ship in the image.** They come from the `server` extra. Moving `httpx` to the `dev` extra would remove three packages, including the only MPL-2.0 package, from the image. The notices list the image as it is today.

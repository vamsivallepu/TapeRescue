### Requirement 2.9: unsupported decoder failure modes

The decoder exposed every observation field on both damaged fixtures, so the patch hook did not move (task 8). These failure modes are still not exercised by any WAV, as recorded in tasks 2.2 and 8:

- a header cut short (partial `header_raw`, `header_crc unreadable`)
- an unreadable header CRC
- a final block with a missing trailer tone
- noise or dropouts inside a payload, as opposed to a cut at the end of the file

The fixtures damage bits through clean FSK, so resynchronisation on real noise has not been tested. Real archival captures are Pending_Item P3.

### Deviation: UEF carrier lengths

The exporter writes `0x0110` carrier chunks of 12240 cycles before block 0 and 2160 cycles before the other blocks, plus a 4800-cycle trailing tone. The design says 5100 and 1800 cycles. Chunk `0x0110` counts cycles at 2400 Hz, so 12240 and 2160 give the BBC timings of 5.1 s and 0.9 s, which match the fixture modulator's `BBC_TIMING`. The design's numbers would give 2.125 s and 0.75 s. The reasoning is in the task 6.5 and 9.4 entries, and the constants are `FIRST_CARRIER_CYCLES` and `OTHER_CARRIER_CYCLES` in `taperescue/uef/exporter.py`. `design.md` has not been changed. Whether the image loads in an emulator is part of P1.
